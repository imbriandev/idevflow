# iOS Learn

Turn real evidence into a smaller, clearer next decision.

## Workflow

1. Gather feedback, observed behavior, metrics, incidents, known issues, and prior learning question.
2. Preserve exact user language where it reveals pain, trust, or value.
3. Separate repeated evidence from isolated requests and founder hypotheses.
4. Decide now, later, not-do, and investigate; explain each decision against the SLC promise.
5. Update product memory, status, retro, or handoff only through authorized writes.
6. Choose one next focus and route to define, plan, build, or test.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn every request into scope.
- Do not cherry-pick feedback to confirm the original bet.
- Do not change target user, monetization, or promise without founder approval.
- Preserve unresolved uncertainty explicitly.

## Output

Report themes, exact language, evidence strength, decisions, next focus, updated document paths, and next command.
