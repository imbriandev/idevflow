# Release and TestFlight Readiness

Load for `/idev:ship`, release metadata, TestFlight readiness, or a candidate Go/No-Go decision. TestFlight is the default target; App Store production is outside this extension’s automatic authority.

## Go/No-Go

Confirm the exact integrated commit, review verdict, fresh non-reused Release build/test receipt, privacy decision, monetization status, version/build/bundle match, known issues, and feedback channel. Require screenshot variants and accessibility/performance proof only when the project enables full release evidence.

Block on unresolved critical/high privacy or security findings, missing primary flow evidence, invalid artifacts, stale source/config/toolchain proof, required purchase/restore evidence gaps, or a candidate whose integration commit moved.

## Manual boundary

Founder approval is bound to the exact candidate. iDevFlow may then promote locally, archive, export, authenticate through the approved credential bridge, and upload one internal beta to App Store Connect. It never pushes Git, selects testers, or distributes; those remain manual next steps.

For a production App Store request, stop and obtain an explicit separate plan/approval covering storefront metadata, screenshots, privacy nutrition labels, pricing/IAP, review notes, rollout, and rollback/mitigation. Do not reinterpret a TestFlight approval as production authority.

## Output

Report Go/No-Go, exact candidate and target, source/verification evidence, data/trust summary, monetization state, blockers/accepted risks, known issues, and remaining founder steps. Never call an app “shipped” merely because the build was uploaded.
